The studio

I'm Ben Strawbridge. I build websites for artists and bands. Along with my wife Natalie, we work to create sites that are fast, personal, and permanent.

The big platforms like WordPress, Squarespace, and GoDaddy are built for volume: thousands of sites on shared templates, with your content locked inside their system. We do the opposite. Every site we build is custom, yours to keep, and free from monthly platform fees.

We are also a website rescue service. If your hosting company shuts down, your CMS is outdated, or you're paying too much for a site you don't love, get in touch. See how site rescue works.
