Ten minutes here saves an hour on our call. Rough answers are fine — estimates and “I don’t know” are both useful. Nothing you put down is binding.

Before you start — what to expect

  • The first conversation is free. I’ll review this worksheet, talk with you for about 45 minutes, and send back a written plan with real numbers.
  • Most projects run $5,000–$8,000 for my work, plus roughly $1,000–$2,500 paid directly to the scanning lab. Smaller collections cost less; we’ll size yours together.
  • Plan on 3–6 months end to end. Most of that is lab turnaround and your family’s time, not mine.
  • You never ship anything to me. I help you choose a lab and write the spec; your originals go straight there and come straight back to you.
  • The work happens in stages, and you approve the price for each one before it starts. You can stop at any stage and still keep everything finished so far.
